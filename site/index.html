        How your game is protected
        
    
        
      
        Your browserlocks everything
        
      
        →
        
      
        Relay serverpasses on locked boxes, cannot open them
        
      
        →
        
      
        Your friends' browsersunlock
        
    
        
    
        Why a relay server? Browsers cannot connect to each other directly. So your
      game goes through a relay server on the internet, a bit like a post office.
        
    
        Locked before it leaves. Your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox …) locks
      everything before it sends it: the game data, your name, the room name. Only the browsers of the
      players in your room get the key. The relay server only passes the locked boxes on and cannot
      read anything. Nobody can change a box on the way unnoticed, or send it again.
        
    
        New keys every time. The browsers agree on new keys whenever somebody enters
      the room. Even someone who records everything cannot open it later.
        
    
        With a password nobody without it can get into the conversation.
      Without a password the room name is the starting point: a relay server that
      guesses the name could secretly put itself in between and read along. A room name from
      Random (like "amber-otter-cloud-river-42") cannot be guessed and protects
      as well as a password; you can still read it out to your friends.
        
    
        The safety code shows that. Everybody in the room sees a code of 12 digits.
      Compare it, e.g. on the phone or in a chat: the same code for everybody means nobody sits in
      between. A different code: leave the room and use a password.
        
    
        The relay server still sees that somebody is in a room, when and how much
      is sent, but not what. Technical details
